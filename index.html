Alice

Your own agent. On your iPhone.

A native iPhone app for your own Hermes agent: talk to it, watch it work and let it run errands for you. Hermes and your model keys stay on your own Mac or server.

Status: a personal project in active daily use and development. It is not on the App Store; you build it with Xcode. It needs iOS 26 and a Hermes you run yourself.

Why it exists

  • You see the page the agent is on, live, and can take over.
  • Logins, cards, keys and verification codes are typed into secure sheets and go to Hermes' vault on your Mac, never into the chat.
  • An irreversible step, such as paying, waits for one explicit “Pay” from you.

You need

  • A Mac or server running Hermes 0.21.x, with a model provider configured.
  • A Mac with Xcode 26, XcodeGen and an Apple developer account.
  • An iPhone with iOS 26 that can reach Hermes, on the same network or through Tailscale.
