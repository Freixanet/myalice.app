Alice

An AI assistant that finishes the job.

Alice is an AI-native iPhone assistant with its own agent system. You ask once — it does the research, runs the errand, verifies the result, and reports back.

Real-world errands

From finding the right product to completing a purchase end-to-end, with hardened price verification before anything is paid.

Proactive briefings

A morning briefing with your agenda, mail that needs action, deadlines, and your sleep — without opening five apps.

Editorial feed

A personal daily edition generated for you, replacing the generic RSS reader.

Guardrails, not vibes

Deterministic verification layers, secret handling, and CI around the AI — so "done" means done.

How it's built

Alice runs a multi-agent system ("Hermes") on the user's own machine:

  • Native iPhone app with chat, voice, and widgets
  • Agent engine with tool use, verification, and approvals
  • Continuous integration: hundreds of tests per change
